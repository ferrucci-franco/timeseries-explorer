# Time Series Explorer v0.8.0

Two new analyses — **Curve fit** on time series and **Trend** — and a rule that every analysis now follows: it computes **off the page**, shows a **computing pill with a Cancel button**, keeps the previous result on screen while it works, and gives the same numbers on a file opened in memory-saving mode as on one in memory, over every row. The **gridded map** gets a desktop layout of its own and real **ERA5** examples over Argentina, **OpenModelica / Dymola** results open in memory-saving mode, signals can be **heard** and exported as WAV, and an **animated guide** shows the features that are easy to miss.

## Downloads

- **Windows 10/11 x64:** installer and portable EXE.
- **macOS Intel:** DMG installer and ZIP package.
- **macOS Apple silicon:** DMG installer and ZIP package.
- **Linux x86_64:** DEB installer and AppImage package.
- **SHA256SUMS files:** checksums for verifying the downloaded packages.

The executables are currently unsigned. Verify that the file was downloaded from the official Time Series Explorer download button and compare its SHA-256 checksum before running it.

- **Windows:** SmartScreen may show an **Unknown publisher** warning. Choose *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** Gatekeeper refuses an unsigned, un-notarized app with *"Time Series Explorer" cannot be opened*. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the message naming the app, and click **Open Anyway**; then confirm in the dialog that follows. On macOS Sequoia this panel is the only route — right-click → Open no longer offers the override.

## New analyses

- **Curve fit for time series.** A line, a parabola, a power law, an exponential or a first-order (step) response fitted to stretches of a signal, each with its own model and range, with the fitted curve drawn and **extrapolated**. A fit, or its **residual**, can be saved as a new signal; the results have a **Copy** button. The 2D curve fit gains the exponential and first-order models, and a double click on a 2D plot fits every pair.
- **Trend.** The mean and the trend of each stretch of a signal: bands whose edges move by pointer, by finger and by key, an optional exponential model under its own tick box, and Zoom / Remove from its toolbar.

## Every analysis can be cancelled

- While an analysis recomputes, a **computing pill** shows over its pane; the plot underneath stays usable and the **previous result stays drawn**.
- **Cancel** stops the work and puts back both the previous result **and its settings** — the options panel and the range never say one thing and show another.
- Every analysis runs on a **background worker**, on files in memory as on memory-saving files: no click on an analysis holds the page for more than 250 ms, and CI measures this on a 1.2 M-row file.
- A newer request stops the query it replaces instead of queueing behind it.

## Files of any size

- **Curve fits** on a memory-saving file use **every row**, past any cap, with the same numbers bit for bit as in memory.
- **FFT** streams the rows so a range change never freezes the page; *Full* reads the whole file.
- The **integral** reads every row on any time axis, through the same kernel as in memory.
- **Σ** (panel statistics), the **Samples** and **Repeated** marks read the rows of the file, not the overview.
- **Save project** on a memory-saving file offers a view; a file with no time column draws the file's rows.
- The **cross-correlation** tool says at once when a file is past its row budget instead of measuring it first.
- On a long signal **in memory**, zoom, pan and autoscale use a block min/max index built while the page is idle.

## Files and examples

- **OpenModelica / Dymola** results (`.mat`) open in memory-saving mode.
- **ERA5 over Argentina:** real reanalysis examples, including the whole of August 2024, in the Examples menu. An example opens in a tab of its own.
- The welcome screen has **Examples** and **Animated guide** buttons beside *Select File*.
- CSV parsing preview: an **Update preview** button, and no confirmation for an edit you have not seen.

## Gridded map

- On desktop the map opens **in a tab of its own** ("Map 1", …), with its controls in the side panel and each extracted series plotted in a new tab named after the place.
- A click **pins** the inspected cell; the right-click menu copies it. Escape goes back to Navigate.
- On a phone the controls answer a tap while **Play** runs.

## Sound

- **Play** the visible window of a signal, on memory-saving files too, and **export it as a WAV** file.
- Phone: sound in the Plot sheet, through the iPhone's silent switch, without overlaps.

## Interface

- **Galleries of cards** for the Plot type, the Analysis menu (time series and 2D) and the Data tools.
- **Animated guide:** scripted demos of the plot types, the map, comparing runs of a simulation and more; captions read at a glance.
- **Legend menu:** icons before every action, the trace's **colour** (a picker of the app's own, which also fixes a browser crash on the phone) and its place among the others.
- **Hover boxes:** one readout rule with SI prefixes and four digits. Long names fit every plot: hover lines wrap and axis titles shorten.
- **Top bar:** full screen, a lower bar, switches that show when they are pressed; Settings labels say what they do.
- One range section, one side panel and one scrollbar per analysis, at one readable size.
- Phone: Σ, Export, Sound and **Share** (to the device's share sheet) in the Plot sheet; every button answers the press; the only plot can be removed.
- Renaming a plotted variable updates its Y axis title.
